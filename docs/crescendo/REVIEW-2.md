# CRESCENDO design review (v7.22, play.html, Chromium 393x852 with real art; spot-check 375x667)

*(Second design review, 2026-11-28, v7.22, real card art, Chromium 393×852 + 375×667 spot-check. The shots lived in a session scratch dir and are gone: regenerate with `tools/ui-drive.js --art --shots DIR` and the circuit/flow drives.)*

Shots: review/shots/ (F1-F4, F9 x4, C1-C4, C6-C8, table, one played game g-*.png, SE copies *-se.png). Contact sheets: review/sheets/.
Not covered: WebKit (Chromium only), C5/F5-F8 plates other than the lost-game pad, sound/motion (stills only).
Played: Iyslander vs Viserai AI, seed 11, to the end (lost on turn 6, ~80 s, bot play).

1. TABLE (mid-game, all states) - MUST. Roughly 45 percent of the 852 px screen (y 250-700) is an empty dark plain whenever nothing is mid-glimpse; both boards are crammed into ~30 px-wide cards at the top and middle-bottom edges. Equipment/arsenal/pitch rows read as thumbnails: no card name, no text, unreadable at arm's length. Why: the cards are "the stars" (BRIEF), yet at rest the stage shows the least. Fix: spend the dead band on the boards (larger arena cards, ~56 px) or put the last-played card/field there; a lane is not "clean" if it is empty.
2. TABLE hit/block state - MUST. "ANY BLOCKS?" plus "HIT FOR 8 / 8 - 0" sit at 10-12 px mono at the right edge, far from the thumb and tiny next to a 150 px attacker card. The single most important number of the turn (damage you will take) is the smallest text on screen. Fix: make "HIT FOR n" a 40+ px numeral under the attacker card, centred, in life-red.
3. TABLE end of game - MUST. Life shows "-1" (red) in the bout bar and on the pad. Life cannot sensibly read below 0. Fix: clamp the display to 0 (keep the true value on the pad only if the rules say so).
4. TABLE over plate "DAWN BREAK / VISERAI WINS" - MUST. A loss is announced in the opponent's name, with no "you"; the win and loss moments should be asymmetric and personal ("You fell", "You win"), and the whole screen behind is dimmed so heavily the finish cannot be studied. The word "Dawn Break" is not explained. Fix: "YOU LOST - Viserai wins on life" / "VICTORY", keep the board 40 percent visible.
5. RESULT PAD (F7) - MUST. After the best moment of the product (a handwritten scorepad, wonderful), the pad's Viserai column is blank, the loser's page shows "-1" with a 3 crossed out; no turns, no life summary, no "what to try next" and no streak/mark line. Three equal-weight bottom actions (New opponent / Leave / Rematch) plus "Saved to your record on this phone" sits at 14 px grey over the dimmed board. Fix: fill both columns (the opponent's life trail), add one coach line ("Your Ice Bolt was held 3 turns"), a record/streak chip, and hide the board behind a stronger scrim.
6. C2 HEROES (list, sheet, empty) - MUST. Hero avatars in the list and in the book grid (24 foe circles) render as pale, washed grey crops (several appear as blank white cards) while C6 and F3 show full-colour portraits of the same heroes. Same hero, three looks. Fix: use the F3 portrait crop everywhere; never a desaturated placeholder.
7. F2 WAYS "The Circuit" row - MUST. Its icon slot is an empty dark square (x 427-475) beside real art/icons on the other four rows. Looks like a missing asset. Fix: give it an icon (the road/ladder glyph used in C6).
8. F2/F1 information scent - SHOULD. Step label "STEP 1 OF 3" on F2 is wrong for The Circuit, Two Phones and Find an Opponent (they are not a 3-step path); the Dummy row is highlighted gold as if selected/default. Fix: drop the step counter on F2 or make it conditional; highlight only the last-used way.
9. F4 SIT DOWN - SHOULD. The dice (≈65 px) sits across the "TAP TO SWAP" pill at 375x667 (shot f4-se) and collides with "THE ROLL / YOU GO FIRST". "TAP TO SWAP" / "TAP TO CHANGE" pills are ~26 px tall (< 44). Fix: move dice to the divider centre-left, make pills 44 px hit areas (invisible padding).
10. F3 HEROES grid - SHOULD. Three columns of 120x165 tiles show name only; no class, no difficulty/size cue, and the last-played is flagged by a 9 px "LAST" chip. At 375x667 only ~3 rows (9 of 17 heroes) are visible with no scroll hint. Fix: add a subtle bottom fade + count ("17 heroes"), put a 12 px class line under the name.
11. C1 YOU - SHOULD. The page opens on a 1248 rating with a sparkline whose y-axis is two tiny labels (1208 / 1248) and "OVER 10 GAMES" at 9-10 px, while the delta pill "+40" is bright. Personal bests "FASTEST WIN" cards are cut at the bottom on first view. Fix: add a faint baseline at 1200 and show "next: #11 is 7 pts away" - gives a reason to play one more game tonight.
12. C1 NEW / C2 EMPTY / C3 EMPTY (the sunrise empty state) - SHOULD. Three different empty screens use the same sun glyph and the same wording ("starts here", "not yet"); two end with the bottom CTA while C2's empty state has none and still lists 17 rows below "UNTRIED". Fix: unique copy per screen and one consistent CTA position; for C2 show the 17 heroes as "Try one" with a tap straight to F4.
13. C3 GAMES - SHOULD. Filter chips (ALL, AI, PEOPLE, RANKED, DAILY, +1) clip at the right edge with no fade; the 6th chip is half visible and is not discoverable. Row subtitle packs 4 facts at ~10 px mono ("SHARP AI · TURN 6 · 11 LEFT · 15:38"): "11 LEFT" is jargon (life left). Fix: fade the chip row, say "won with 11 life", move the time to the right.
14. C3 DETAIL sheet - SHOULD. "Same decks, same shuffle, same coin. A practice game: it does not change your record." is 12 px grey under a primary button; "HOW IT ENDED / You won on life" etc. are 9 px labels at the left with the value 20 px at the right (huge gap, eyes travel). Fix: tighten label/value pairs, 12 px min.
15. C4 LADDER - SHOULD. The header paragraph ("Ranked server tables only. The best 50 by rating are listed; a player name and 3 ranked games put you on it. Friendly tables never count.") is 12 px, three lines, ahead of YOUR PLACE; the player's own row is the real answer. Rows are 52 px high with 1411 right-aligned in 24 px - good - but no hero icon or form (W/L streak), so every row looks alike. Fix: shorten to one line, move rules into an (i) sheet, add a 3-dot form strip.
16. C4 FAIL - SHOULD. "THE LADDER IS NOT ANSWERING" error card is pink-outlined with a ~14 px red wifi-off glyph; the sun glyph used elsewhere is replaced by nothing, and the page below is dead space. OK copy, but it does not reassure: say what still works ("Your record is safe on this phone. You can still play.") and make TRY AGAIN secondary to PLAY.
17. C6 CIRCUIT home - SHOULD. "TODAY'S CHALLENGE NOT PLAYED YET" plus "NEW IN 8 H 19 M" - the reset line at 9 px is the best return-tomorrow hook in the app and is the smallest text. Streak is shown only as dots; no flame, no "day N" number. The header "ROAD · DAILY · LADDER" repeats the tab names. Fix: show "Day 3 streak" 18 px under the card title and make the reset timer 12 px.
18. C7 ROAD - SHOULD. "0 OF 17 CLEARED" progress bar is 6 px tall and nearly black-on-black; "ARMORY 0 OF 5" tier label and "SKIRMISH" use a different weight from the card headings; "NOT YET" under 16 rows repeats 16 times. Fix: replace per-row "NOT YET" with a lock glyph, brighten the progress bar to 10 px, gold only the next rung.
19. C8 DAILY - SHOULD. Two art cards (Gravy Bones vs Fai) read well, but the three explainer lines (calendar / deck / flag icons, 12 px) and the week strip (S M T W T F S in 28 px boxes, "-" glyph) share equal weight; "NO RUN TODAY · BEST 0 · 0 WON OF 0 PLAYED" at 10 px is a wall of zeros on a first visit. Copy "Same deal for everyone today" is warm - keep; cut the zero line when nothing played.
20. F9 WAITING (server/phone/fail/quiet) - SHOULD. The sunrise is gorgeous but the quiet panel (f9quiet) floats mid-screen with the dim page's top bar missing (top 60 px is a flat black band, no back arrow) - looks like a layout slip. "WE COULD NOT REACH THE SERVER" wraps to two lines at 28 px and the unfriendly "WE COULD NOT" voice is not the coach's. Fix: "The server did not answer." and keep the back arrow in the quiet state.
21. CROSS-SCREEN CONSISTENCY - SHOULD. Three different small-caps eyebrows (mono 10 px "KEPT ON THIS PHONE", "MASTERY, FROM YOUR SIDE", "EVERY ONE, FROM YOUR SIDE") sit above headings as gray 9-10 px text; on the phone they are close to the 11 px floor the drives enforce and are the lowest contrast text (about 3:1). Fix: raise to 11-12 px and +1 step of luminance.
22. CROSS-SCREEN BUTTONS - NICE. Primary cream pill (PLAY, SIT DOWN, SHUFFLE UP, END TURN) is consistent, but secondary dark pills ("STOP LOOKING", "LEAVE THE TABLE", "NEW OPPONENT") have almost no contrast against the bg (~1.3:1 fill), so they read as disabled. Fix: 1 px light border or a lighter fill.
23. TABLE paying state (g-02) - NICE. Cost is shown as two empty circles and "PAID 0/2" in pink mono 12 px; the primary reads "PITCH 2 MORE" disabled (good), but a first-timer does not learn that the HAND is where to tap. Fix: pulse the ready hand cards once, or add "tap a card to pitch it" under the counter for the first 3 games.
24. TABLE bottom dock - NICE. "..." and the pad icon are 40 px circles (below the 44 pt rule) flanking a 52 px primary; at 375x667 the hand row is 108 px and the arsenal/pitch rows are the only legible thing. Fix: 44 px circles and an icon label for the pad.
25. WHOLE GAME LOOP - NICE. A full game played in ~80 s (bot, but the AI paced fine) with no refused taps visible; friction was after it: no "one more" hook in the pad (finding 5) and the pad "TAP TO CONTINUE" on the plate gives no hint that the result was recorded until the next screen. Fix: show the recorded chip on the plate itself and offer "Rematch" there.

Things that are good, keep: the F1 title lockup and the hero crop, F9 sunrise, the lined-pad result card with "GG" stamp, C4 standings row typography, 375x667 holds up (no clipping, buttons stay on screen), copy on C8 ("Same deal for everyone today").
