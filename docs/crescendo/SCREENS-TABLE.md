## 1. Deviations from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md (and why)

- **[DEV-1] The mat card is 44×61 (48×67 at 430), not 56×78.** DS §10.2 budgeted two or three zones per half; a real hero fields weapon + 4 equipment + graveyard + deck + arsenal = **8 zones**, and a pitch pile, tokens and a hand fan on top. 8 columns × 56 does not fit in 373 px. 44 is also the tap floor, so 8 slots of 44 + 3 gap = exactly 373. The mat is where tidiness beats size; the stars (hand 80×112, chain 112×156, glimpse 232×324) are untouched.
  **The lane = one row of 8 FIXED columns, identical for both seats** (same fact, same place; not mirrored): x = 10 + 47·k → 1 weapon (10) · 2 head (57) · 3 chest (104) · 4 arms (151) · 5 legs (198) · 6 graveyard (245) · 7 deck (292) · 8 arsenal (339). Empty column = bare mat (no dashed slot), except the arsenal drop slot in T9. More than 5 equipment/weapon items (second weapon, tokens, auras): extra items stack onto column 5 with a ×N hex counter badge; tokens of one kind are one card.
  **Pitch zone** = this turn's pitched cards fan out of the deck pile (16 px, then +8 px each, toward the marker row), face up, up to 3 + a count; CR 4.4.3c sends them to the deck bottom at end phase, so "out of the deck and back" is literally the rules. Tapping the deck column opens them (only while non-empty).
  **Marker row (33 px at 393)**: your/their AP gems and resource coins at the left (20 / 22 px, only when non-zero), their hand as a fan of backs 22×31 at 11 px step centred (display only). Neither is tappable. Sizes per band in §14.
- **[DEV-2] A used (tapped) weapon lies sideways at 72 % scale** (rotate 90°, scale .72, stamps counter-rotated) so it keeps its 44 px column; the tap target stays the whole 44×61 column.
- **[DEV-3] The announcer slab sits at y 60 (just under the bout), 60 h, z 72 (above the scrim)** instead of the stage top edge: the 324 px glimpse needs y 126–450, and slab + card + gap (60+4+6) only fit between bout and tray if the slab goes first. One position for every announcement (same event, same place).
- **[DEV-4] "Any blocks?" and the step marker share one slot** top-left of the stage (22 px / 18 px high). The attack card is centred 3 px low so the slot is free.
- **[DEV-5] At compact (548) the step marker moves top-right** (the attack card leaves only 22 px of free height and the marker would overlap it).
- Hub secondary left becomes **✕ cancel** (44×44) while paying; right is empty during inspect. Both are the same slots.

## 2. Universal rules

**Taps.** Every tappable ≥ 44×44 (verified, §15). A press paints within a frame (`:active` + `touchstart` class, no transition), action fires on pointer-up (MOTION §3.1). A refused tap: the card gets the `--refused` ring + reason chip ("NEEDS 2 MORE"), nudge ±4 px 140 ms. Never a silent tap.
**Inspect.** Tap or hold on any face-up card on the table = inspect (T10). In the hand, tap = select; hold (400 ms) = inspect. Own face-down arsenal: tap = select (play from arsenal) or hold = peek. Their face-down cards (hand, deck, arsenal) are never tappable.
**Manners.** Ranked/tournament shows only Visible/Public/Unverified information (TRP 5.1): both life totals, all face-up cards, counts of piles, step/phase, AP/resources, this turn's pitch. Never: their hand faces, their arsenal face, deck order, a card after it left a public zone (no log). Their hand is backs whose COUNT is public; their arsenal is a back with no dot (your own arsenal shows a jade count dot).
**Hand states.** *ready* = lifted 4 px with a 3 px jade underline (you can act with it now); *dim* = flat, brightness .58 (you have priority and cannot play it); *idle* = flat, normal (you do not have priority: no underline, no dim, so the whole row is not greyed on their turn); *selected* = lift 12, scale 1.04, 2 px bone ring, name-plate; *blocker* = same lift with a 2 px silver ring and a silver shield badge.
**Name-plate.** Hoisted out of the hand scroller (a scroller clips) onto the stage layer, bottom at y 456 (= tray top 458 − 2); centred on the card, or left-aligned to the card when it would leave the screen (card 0). Not tappable (inspect is hold).
**One primary button**: 265×52 at (64,600), bone. Label = the line you would say. Fit rule: start 20 px; step down by 1 to 15 px until it fits one line; below 15 use two lines at 14. Disabled = outline + `--ink-2` text.
