## What shipped
- **Lyath is back.** A fresh misplay census across every hero found that v6.15's "use the weapon bonus you set
  up" fix had made the basic AI badly worse with Lyath: 7.42 → 3.08 wins of 28, down on every seed. Her Might
  token gives +1 to ANY next attack, and the AI counted that as a reason to swing her 3-cost weapon into blocks
  it could not beat. Now a bonus lifts a weapon only when no card in hand could carry it just as well. Lyath is
  restored (8.00 = 8.00, and 7.33 → 7.42 over 12 seeds). Arakni's and Dorinthea's v6.15 gains hold, and the other
  twelve heroes play identically.

## What we learned
- v6.15 was measured only on the heroes it targeted. From now on an AI change is measured on every hero it can
  affect, each in its own seat.
