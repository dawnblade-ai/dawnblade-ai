# Progress report — v6.27 (2026-11-14)

## What shipped
- **The other phone now hears your move at once.** Your two-phone test worked but moves took up to about 5 seconds. The cause: in every
  browser the page asked the wrong address for the small helper script that opens the live connection, so the live connection never
  opened and each phone only learned of the other's move by asking now and then. Fixed; measured on the real page against the real
  service: the other phone's move arrives in about a quarter of a second (median 234 ms, slowest 5% under 475 ms).
- **A safety net:** if the live connection is ever down, the phone now asks the table every 2 seconds (about 1.4 seconds to see a move,
  2 s at worst), and a host's lobby no longer stays shut. It costs one small request per 2 s per phone only while the connection is down.
- **Why the checks missed it:** every test handed the page its helper ready-made. There is now a check that makes the page load it the
  real way, and a note in the lessons file.
- The table's web function now reports its own timings (not visible to players) so the next slowness can be located quickly.

## Not known
- Why your game saw "up to 5 seconds" rather than about 20: with no live connection the wait should have been longer (or the lobby
  should not have opened). Not explained.
- A real phone's connection to the live service (the real page was driven in a desktop browser through the build sandbox's proxy).

2. Tap *Find an opponent*, then *Join a server table*; one phone taps *Open a server table* and shares the six-letter code, the other taps
   *Enter a table code*, types it and taps *Sit down*.
3. Choose heroes, throw, and play a few turns.

Report back: roughly how many seconds from your tap until the other phone showed your move (and the reverse), any banner text
(for example "reconnecting" or "not answering") and when, and the phone and browser.
