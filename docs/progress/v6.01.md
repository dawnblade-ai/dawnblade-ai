## What's next — hidden hands (user: "a must")
Design on `research/hidden-hands` (`HIDDEN-HANDS.md`): one **referee** module holds the full state and
sends each phone only its own view. Spike: 13,231 of 13,231 self-play actions were legal from the
actor's own view.
- **H1** view layer (redaction, per-seat feed) · **H2** host-as-referee — first shippable: the guest
  sees nothing hidden · **H3** a neutral referee (third device or free-tier server) · **H4** the ranked
  Elo ladder, rock-paper-scissors for the first turn, the referee as result authority.
