# Progress report — v6.33 (2026-11-15)

## What shipped
- **A ranked game nobody comes back to can be claimed.** If the player a ranked game is waiting on makes no move for five minutes, the other player sees **Claim the win**. Tap it and the
  game ends as a win by forfeit: the absent player loses, both ratings move the usual way, and both end screens say it was a forfeit. Until the clock runs out the table keeps their seat.
- **A calm countdown.** The player the game is waiting on sees how long they have, but only in the last two minutes ("The game is waiting on you. Rival One may claim the win in 1:42.").
  The other player sees how long until they may claim after about a minute of waiting. Neither screen says anything about what is being decided.
- **Closing a ranked table counts.** Once a ranked game has started, closing the table is a concession: the page tells you so first ("Closing now counts as a loss…"), and afterwards how your
  rating moved. Closing before the game starts, and any friendly table, is free and unchanged.
- **Recovery keeps your rating.** If you recover your old player on a new phone that had already played a ranked game, that phone's own record is no longer thrown away (it is kept, unused);
  your recovered player's rating and record are untouched.

## Not known / not built yet (the honest list)
- **Not deployed.** One new SQL file (`007_forfeit.sql`, run it first) and a new function version. The live check was written but has not run on the real project, and it does not play a
  ranked game (that would move two real ratings), so a real five-minute forfeit has only been proven offline.
- **The five minutes is a placeholder** (one constant). In the defend step the game waits on the attacker, who holds priority; an attacker who sits on priority while the defender picks
  blockers could be claimed against after the limit.
- A ranked game neither player finishes just ages out with the table after two days and records nothing.
- Nothing stops two friends farming rating by conceding on turn one; a minimum-length rule is still a question for the user.
- No leaderboard screen yet.
