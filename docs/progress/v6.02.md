# Progress report — v6.02 (2026-11-07)

**Mission (user, 2026-11-07):** the ultimate FaB Silver Age precon competitive gaming environment and
judging engine — 100% fan-made, non-profit, for fun and glory, no ads, forever.

This report covers one CEO thread: **v6.01 → v6.02** (2026-11-07), the first wave of **hidden hands**
(user ruling 2026-11-07: "Hidden hands is a must"). One Opus worker built it; the CEO re-validated it on
the merged tree before it shipped.

## What shipped — v6.02, Hidden hands H1: the view, for real (engine only, no new screens)
- **Each seat can now be shown only what it may know.** `view(state, seat)` hides the opponent's hand,
  face-down arsenal, intimidated cards and face-down gear, the order of both decks, and the random seed
  (which would let a phone compute future draws). Hidden cards become blank placeholders whose ids say
  nothing about the decklist. What went public while still in a hand (declared blockers, pitched cards,
  the card being paid for) stays visible.
- **The AI is held to the same standard.** Sharp's lookahead now starts from that seat's view. Where the
  old lookahead peeked (mostly at the soul), it no longer does.
- **Private looks stay private.** Compass's look and Reload's face-down arsenal no longer sit in shared
  state or name the card in the shared feed; feed lines carry who may read them.
- **Every field that copies a card has a visibility rule**, and a drill fails when a new one appears
  without one.

## How it was proven
- New drills over self-play for all 15 heroes, each sabotaged and caught: swapping hidden cards never
  changes the view; the AI chooses the same move from its view as from the full board (0 misses); every
  move made from a seat's own view is legal (0 refusals); no feed line names a card hidden from its reader.
- Suite: 3,991 pass, 0 fail, 5 skipped. Scenes 107/0. Fairness: nothing found. Both page blocks compile.
- Self-play ladder: 630 games, 0 policy refusals, 0 invariant violations, 0 stalls; win counts identical
  to v6.01.
- Sharp vs v6.01 on its own seat (3 seeds): −0.09 wins of 28, noise. Phone budget at 4x CPU throttle:
  p95 77–88 ms, worst 111 ms (limit 150 / 400).
- The real page at phone size: 0 page errors; the same 4 layout FAILs as v6.01 (pre-existing, see QUEUE).

## What's next
- **H2 — hidden hands, casual (the first version players will see):** the host's phone runs the referee;
  the guest's phone receives only its view. The seed and the rock-paper-scissors throw are committed before
  they are revealed, and either player can verify the game afterwards. The host could still look, and the
  game will say so.
- Then **H3**, a neutral referee (third device or a free-tier server), and **H4**, the ranked Elo ladder.
