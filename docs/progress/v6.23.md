# Progress report — v6.23 (2026-11-13)

## What shipped
- **Silver Age legality is recorded.** The card database we pin from says which cards Silver Age bans, and since
  when. A small tool (`tools/silver-age.js`) reads that off the pinned pool for the cards the fifteen decks play and
  writes it beside the format. Nine entries: Reaping Blade (since 2026-05-29); Absorb in Aether, Beaten Trackers,
  Briar (hero), Emeritus Scolding, Lightning Press, Pulping, Sigil of Suffering, Snapback (since 2026-09-18).
  A drill fails if the list ever drifts from the pool.
- **Dawnblade itself is unchanged.** No deck list changed and nothing became illegal in the game.
- **Full re-pin of the card pool.** 38 records moved; none changed the text, cost, power or defence of a card a deck
  plays (Hyper Inflation's yellow and blue powers dropped, and decks play red only). DATA_VER sage-v16 → sage-v17.

## Not known
- The official announcement pages are still unreachable, so the list is the database's word. Hero benches are
  recorded as bans with no end date, because the data carries neither distinction.
