# Progress report — v6.24 (2026-11-13)

## What shipped
- **The simpler AI sees a bonus that is already live.** An attack that prints a small number but pumps itself under a
  condition that is already true (a crowd that has booed, an aura played this turn) used to lose to a plain attack
  with a bigger printed number. The AI now ranks hand attacks by the power they would have if played now.
  The coach says so: "Swing the attack that hits hardest right now."
- **It only counts what it can be sure of.** Conditions that wait on the payment, the chain or the opponent's
  reply count for nothing, so the number is never above what is known. 22 of the 38 conditional pumps the precons
  print can be decided up front; 16 cannot.
- **The engine's own rules are untouched.** The same condition reader now serves both the game and the AI.

## Not known
- Only Lyath clearly gains (rule policy +0.67 of 28, 24 seeds). Every other hero is flat within the noise, and seven
  heroes play identically. Sharp is flat to slightly up (Lyath +1.33 over 6 seeds, not resolved).
- The phone timing check at 4x CPU throttle was not re-run; unthrottled search cost is unchanged.
