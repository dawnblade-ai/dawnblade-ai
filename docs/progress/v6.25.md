# Progress report — v6.25 (2026-11-13)

## What shipped
- **A card that needs a target cannot be played without one.** The user confirmed the rule. Scuttle Toes' ability,
  which used to cost resources and then do nothing when you had no ally, is now refused up front.
- **The first piece of the online table.** A referee that runs on a server, one action at a time, was built and
  drilled offline (no hosting account yet). It keeps the deck and the seed secret, and sends each player only their
  own view. One action takes about 20 ms of its own work on this machine.
- **A hidden card could be named by a used-flag.** When a card with a once-per-turn ability went back to its owner's
  hand, the other player's screen could be told which hidden card it was. The other player's view no longer carries
  that mark for a card they cannot see.

## Not known
- The server's network round trips, the realtime channel's size limit and its access rules were not measured: no
  account was available. Only the offline numbers exist.
- Four cards print effects beside their target that do not need it (Preserve Tradition, Pass Over, A Drop in the
  Ocean, Edict of Steel); they are refused whole without a target. Waiting on the user to confirm that reading.
